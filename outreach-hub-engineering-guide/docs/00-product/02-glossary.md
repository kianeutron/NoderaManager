    # Domain Glossary

    Use these terms consistently in code, UI, database, tests, API, and MCP.

- **Route**: top-level acquisition strategy, e.g. Agency Overflow, Recruiters, Referral Partners.
- **Module**: narrower tactic/category inside a Route.
- **Person**: a human contact with stable identity information.
- **Organization**: company, agency, consultancy, recruiter, community, or other organization.
- **Prospect**: a qualification record connecting a Person/Organization to an outreach opportunity and strategy context.
- **Signal**: evidence that makes contact timely, e.g. live role, client win, contractor request.
- **Campaign**: a bounded batch/initiative with a goal, time window, and targeting rules.
- **Outreach**: an outbound contact attempt such as email or LinkedIn InMail.
- **Interaction**: inbound or outbound communication after initial outreach, including replies.
- **Follow-up**: a planned future action tied to a prospect/contact.
- **Persona**: functional buyer/referral category such as recruiter, fractional CTO, agency founder.
- **Response depth**: normalized stage describing commercial significance of a response.
- **Warm lead**: contact that has shown concrete future or current commercial interest.
- **Structural no**: rejection caused by a persistent constraint such as language or residency.
- **Timing no**: no current need while fit remains plausible.
- **Document**: metadata record for an uploaded/library item.
- **Blob**: stored binary object in the file provider.
- **Audit event**: immutable record of a material mutation.
